# That Friend: friends beta guide

Thanks for trying That Friend! It's a group trip planner. You can use it on
the web or entirely by text, and your job is to try to break it.

## What to try

Pick a real trip you're thinking about, or make one up:

1. **Start a trip.** Either on the web, or by texting the That Friend number
   something like "lisbon in march".
2. **Invite 2–3 of us.** Send the link, or text the numbers to That Friend.
3. **Mark which days you're free** and see whether the suggested dates make
   sense.
4. **Forward stuff to That Friend by text:** a Google Maps link, a TikTok,
   an Instagram post, a screenshot of a restaurant. Check that each one lands
   on the map in the right place.
5. **Ask it questions by text:** "what are we doing saturday?", "who's in?",
   "is this going to be expensive?"
6. **Vote on where to stay,** and close the vote.
7. **Try it the way you'd really use it:** on your phone, in a hurry, with
   bad signal, switching apps halfway through.

## When something's off

Anything counts: a crash, a wrong date, a confusing message, a text you
didn't expect, or just "huh?".

Tell us any way that's easy for you: text us, or
[open a bug report](../../../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). The most
useful things to include:

- **What you did**, step by step if you can.
- **What you expected** and **what happened instead**.
- **A screenshot or screen recording.** On an iPhone: Control Center → Screen
  Recording.
- **Your phone and browser**, e.g. "iPhone 15, opened from iMessage", and
  **where you are**, since timezones matter.
- **The trip name.**

## Good to know

- Texting only works with **US phone numbers** for now.
- Reply **STOP** to That Friend at any time to stop texts. Reply **START** to
  turn them back on.
- It's a beta. Your trips are real, but please don't rely on it for bookings
  you can't redo.
